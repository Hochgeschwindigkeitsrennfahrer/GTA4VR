# 017 — 6DOF hand and melee-weapon combat

## Goal

Make unarmed and melee-weapon attacks respond to tracked hand motion and world-space contact. Depends on the accepted #014 camera/scale, #015/#016 hand transforms and #018 physics foundation. Roadmap stage 6. Start with hand-to-hand combat; knives and other melee weapons follow.

- [x] Recognize a punch independently for each hand when grip and trigger close the four fingers excluding the thumb, and tracked hand motion exceeds a sufficient speed. Do not use thumb input as a punch gate.
- [x] Locate melee hit/damage and animation ownership; establish tracked fist and equipped knife/bat/cue swept collision volumes.
- [x] Use swept contact and minimum motion thresholds for punches/stabs/swings with cooldown/contact rearm; do not cap maximum swing speed.
- [x] Preserve native enemy damage, reactions, blood/impact effects and player attribution without attacker animation taking over IK.
- [x] Deliver vehicle/object strikes through native damage, impact and physics handlers.
- [x] Record user acceptance of punches, knife, bat, cue and world-object strikes.
- [ ] Follow-up in #033: haptics, physical weapon blocking/support grip and broader lifecycle/episode/flat-screen regression coverage.

## Done when
